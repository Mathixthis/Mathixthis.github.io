Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Nextround is an Overwolf app that helps VALORANT players improve. This page explains what data the app uses and what it does with it. The French version of this policy is the reference version.

Your match data

  • While you play, Nextround receives your match data from Overwolf: kills, deaths, abilities, damage, score, agent and map.
  • This data is analysed and stored only on your computer, in the app’s local storage. It covers your last 20 matches and your current goal.
  • It is not sent to any server, and it is never sold or shared. The only exceptions are what you choose to send yourself and crash reports (see below).
  • You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

Messages you send us

  • The “Idea? Bug?” form sends your message, its type (idea, bug, other) and the app version.
  • It sends neither your username, nor your account, nor your stats. Don’t write personal information in the message.
  • Feedback on a tip (👍 or 👎 under your mission): if you click, Nextround sends your feedback, your comment if any, the detected mistake with its figure, and the agent, map, rank and number of rounds of that match. Nothing is sent if you don’t click.
  • Survey (“Does Nextround help you?”, once after 5 matches): your score from 1 to 5 and the number of analysed matches.
  • Automatic crash report: if the app crashes, it sends the technical error message and the version, without username or stats. At most three reports per session.
  • These go through the Nextround relay (a small server of ours), which posts them on our Discord. The relay keeps your IP address in memory for one hour at most to block abuse, without storing it. These messages are only used to improve the app.

Discord sharing (off by default)

  • If you tick “Share on Discord”, Nextround sends to the Nextround Discord server: the display name you chose, unlocked badges, finished challenges and completed missions.
  • These messages also contain your link code (e.g. NR-3NXM92), used only by the Nextround bot to give you your roles if you type /lier.
  • Nothing else is sent: neither your Riot ID nor your detailed stats.
  • Untick the box to stop sharing at any time. To have what was already shared deleted, contact us.

Advertising

  • Nextround shows ads provided by Overwolf.
  • Overwolf and its advertising partners may use cookies and identifiers to show and measure these ads, under their own policy: overwolf.com/legal/privacy.

No account

Nextround asks for no account, no email and no password.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The demo keeps the simulated match in your browser’s storage, without sending anything.
